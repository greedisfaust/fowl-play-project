# Elevator Procedural Visuals

The elevator scene keeps its editor-authored hierarchy. The first visual pass is generated by
`world/elevator/elevator_visuals.gd`, an `@tool` script that creates reusable primitive
MeshInstance3D geometry under the existing visual grouping nodes.

## Attach the visual builder

1. Open `res://world/elevator/elevator.tscn`.
2. Select the root `Elevator` node.
3. In the Inspector, choose **Attach Script**.
4. Set the script path to `res://world/elevator/elevator_visuals.gd`.
5. Keep the script's defaults and save the scene.
6. Allow the editor to finish rebuilding the scene. Generated nodes should appear under
   `Structure/GeneratedShell`, `InteriorDetails/GeneratedInterior`,
   `Lighting/GeneratedLighting`, and each door pivot's `GeneratedPanel`.

The script only generates visuals. It does not replace the authored scene, collision,
interaction framework, or MainController.

## First-pass dimensions and orientation

- Interior target: 3.2 m wide × 3.0 m deep × 2.8 m high.
- Door opening: 2.4 m wide × 2.2 m high.
- Cabin floor top: Y = 0.
- Front entrance: negative Z.
- The four existing door pivots are positioned by the builder at X = -0.9, -0.3, 0.3,
  and 0.9 m, with their panel centers at Y = 1.1 m.

The exterior shell includes wall and ceiling thickness outside the stated interior dimensions.
The visual pass uses primitive BoxMesh pieces and generated StandardMaterial3D resources:
piano-black lacquer, champagne brass, walnut accents, dark inset panels, and warm lighting.

## Rebuilding and editing

- Change the exported finish colors on the Elevator root to adjust the palette.
- Toggle **Rebuild Visuals** in the Inspector to regenerate the generated groups after changing
  exported colors or geometry constants.
- Generated content is grouped under clearly named `Generated...` nodes. Do not put hand-authored
  meshes inside those generated groups because rebuilding replaces their contents.
- Keep custom/editor-authored additions under the existing non-generated parent nodes.

## Current scope

This is the initial geometry and material pass only. Sliding-door animation, elevator buttons,
occupancy tracking, network synchronization, and MainController transition integration are
separate follow-up slices. No runtime or multiplayer behavior is claimed by this visual builder.
